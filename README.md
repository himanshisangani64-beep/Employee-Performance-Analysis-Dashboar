## 📌 Project Overview

This project uses **Microsoft Excel** to analyze employee performance data and explore workforce patterns across departments, job roles, experience levels, salaries, and employee satisfaction.

The **Employee Performance Analysis Dashboard** combines KPI cards, charts, summary tables, and interactive filters to transform employee records into meaningful insights.

It provides a consolidated view of total employees, average experience, average salary, performance ratings, completed projects, training hours, and job satisfaction to support HR reporting and workforce analysis.

## 🎯 Project Objective

- Analyze the total number of employees and overall workforce distribution.
- Evaluate average employee experience and annual salary.
- Understand employee performance ratings across different roles.
- Compare performance across departments.
- Identify employees with the highest project completion.
- Analyze employee job satisfaction levels.
- Explore the relationship between experience and performance ratings.
- Review training-hour distributions across months.
- Build an interactive Excel dashboard for employee performance reporting.

## ✨ Key Features

## 📊 Analysis & Excel Practice

### 1. 👥 Employee Performance Analysis

Used a bar chart to compare employee performance ratings and identify differences in individual performance.

### 2. ⭐ Job Satisfaction Analysis

Used a pie chart to visualize job satisfaction scores for selected employees and explore employee satisfaction patterns.

### 3. 🚀 Project Completion Analysis

Compared completed projects across employees to identify individuals with higher project contributions.

### 4. 📈 Experience vs. Performance Analysis

Used a line chart to explore employee experience in years alongside performance ratings.

### 5. ⏳ Employee Experience Analysis

Compared the experience levels of selected employees to understand workforce experience distribution.

### 6. 📅 Monthly Training-Hour Analysis

Analyzed training hours across different months to understand employee training participation and identify periods with higher training activity.

### 7. 🏢 Department & Job-Level Analysis

Used interactive filters to explore employee data across departments such as Engineering, Finance, HR, Marketing, Operations, and Sales, along with different job levels.

### 8. 📊 KPI & Dashboard Analysis

Combined KPI cards, bar charts, a pie chart, a line chart, summary metrics, and interactive filters into one dashboard for clear employee performance reporting.

## 🔄 From Raw Data to Employee Insights

This project follows a structured workflow to transform employee records into meaningful HR insights.

| **Step** | **Process** | **Purpose** |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Raw Data | Explore employee performance records |
| 02 | Data Preparation | Organize employee, department, salary, and performance details |
| 03 | KPI Analysis | Calculate total employees, average salary, experience, and completed projects |
| 04 | Performance Analysis | Compare employee ratings, job satisfaction, and project completion |
| 05 | Training & Experience Analysis | Explore training hours and experience-related patterns |
| 06 | Data Visualization | Create charts and summary tables |
| 07 | Dashboard Creation | Combine KPIs, charts, and interactive filters |
| 08 | Employee Insights | Identify performance patterns and workforce trends |
